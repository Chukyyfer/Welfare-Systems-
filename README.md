

Welfare Systems

About

Welfare Systems is a community-focused initiative exploring practical ways welfare support can help improve people's lives.

This project includes an interactive survey designed to gather people's views, needs, and experiences around different areas of welfare support.

Survey Areas

The survey explores areas including:

- 🏠 Rental support
- 🏥 Health support
- 🎓 Education support
- 💰 Salary Up-front

Participants can complete the survey and, where applicable, provide information to express interest in a welfare programme.

Purpose

The goal of the survey is to better understand the kinds of support people may need and to create a clearer picture of how welfare systems can serve individuals and communities.

Your response helps contribute to this process.
